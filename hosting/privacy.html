Privacy Policy

For the Cue and Cue Staff apps · Effective 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Cue app (for students and customers) and the Cue Staff app (for canteen staff) handle, why, and the choices you have. Both apps are made by Quicko Software Solutions (“Quicko”, “we”, “us”), which also acts as the data fiduciary for the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short: Cue has no student accounts, no ads, no payments, no location tracking and no analytics. Your token number stays on your phone. Staff accounts use a work email address only so staff can sign in.

What the apps do

A canteen gives you a numbered token when you order. In Cue you type that number to see whether your order is waiting, being prepared, next in line or ready, and you get an alert when it is ready. Canteen staff use Cue Staff to issue tokens, mark orders ready and hand them over.

Information we handle

Cue (student app)

  • Token numbers you track. Saved only on your phone so the app remembers your orders. They are not sent to us and are not linked to you.
  • Your settings (theme, sound, vibration and whether you dismissed the alerts prompt). Saved only on your phone.
  • The live order board. The app reads the canteen’s board, which holds only token numbers and the times each order was created, made ready and collected. It contains no names, phone numbers or other personal details.
  • Notification permission. If you allow notifications, the app shows a local alert on your phone when your order is ready. No push token or contact details are sent to us.
  • A random app instance ID. When the app checks whether an update is required, it sends a random identifier created by the app (not your device ID or advertising ID), the app version and the app identifier to Firebase Remote Config.

Cue does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.

Cue Staff (staff app)

  • Staff account details. Your work email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by Firebase Authentication, never visible to us) and an account ID. Staff accounts are created by the canteen’s administrator; the app cannot create accounts.
  • Order updates. When you issue a token or mark it ready or collected, the app saves the token number and the time. These records do not store which staff member made the change.
  • Settings and the random app instance ID, as described for the student app.

Why we use it

  • To show each order’s live status and alert you when it is ready.
  • To let only approved staff sign in and change the order board.
  • To tell you when an app update is available or required.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, build profiles or share it with data brokers.

Service providers and where data is stored

Both apps run on Google Firebase, operated by Google LLC and its affiliates, which processes data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms.

How long we keep it

  • Order board: an order drops off the active board within 4 hours. Each record is overwritten when its token number is used again or cleared by staff, so no order history is kept.
  • Staff accounts: kept while the person works at the canteen, and deleted when the canteen asks us to remove them or the person requests deletion.
  • Data on your phone: stays until you remove the order, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove a tracked order in Cue at any time with Remove. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Turn notifications, sound and vibration on or off in the app’s Settings or your phone’s settings.
  • Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you and raise a grievance. To ask for any of these, including deleting a staff account and its data, email quickosoftwaresolutions@gmail.com with the subject “Cue data request”. We aim to reply within 7 days and to complete requests within 30 days.
  • If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Cue is meant for college students and canteen staff. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. The student app collects no personal data from anyone. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Access to the order board is controlled by security rules: anyone with the app can read token statuses, and only signed-in, approved staff accounts can change them. No method is perfectly secure, but we use Google’s managed infrastructure and limit what we collect to keep risk low.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will let users know before it takes effect.
